An intern swore this file was "encrypted" before they left. It isn't — it's
just been run through a couple of common encodings, one stacked on top of the
other.

We pulled the blob out of a chat log and saved it as cipher.txt. The character
set is the giveaway for the outer layer: only A-Z, a-z, 0-9, + and / with a
trailing = pad. Peel that off and you'll be staring at the next layer, which is
shorter and made up entirely of the digits 0-9 and the letters a-f.

Strip the encodings layer by layer until you reach printable ASCII. The flag
looks like FLAG{...}.

Tools like base64, xxd, and python3 are all you need — no key, no brute force.
